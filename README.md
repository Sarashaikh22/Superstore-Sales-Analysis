# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes sales, profit, and overall business performance using the Superstore dataset. The goal is to identify key drivers of revenue, profitability, and efficiency across categories, regions, and customers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit trends over time
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories
- Evaluate regional performance and profit margins
- Detect loss-making or low-margin customers
- Understand factors impacting profitability (discounts, costs)

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- SQL (SQLite) – Data analysis
- Power BI – Data visualization & dashboard

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders
- Sales & Profit by Category
- Profit Margin by Category & Region
- Sales Trend over Time
- Top Customers by Sales
- Interactive Filters (Region, Category, Segment)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Technology category generates the highest profit with strong margins
- Furniture shows lower profitability, likely due to higher discounts
- Sales and profit are increasing over time, indicating business growth
- Profit margin shows a slight decline in the latest year, suggesting reduced efficiency
- Some high-revenue customers contribute low or negative profit

---

## 📁 Files Included
- `Superstore_Sales_Analysis.pdf` – Dashboard export
- `superstore.csv` – Dataset
- `README.md` – Project documentation

---

## 🚀 Conclusion
This analysis highlights the importance of evaluating both revenue and profitability. While the business is growing, declining margins and loss-making customers indicate areas for improvement.
